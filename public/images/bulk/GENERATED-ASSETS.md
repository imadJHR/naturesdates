## Prompt specification

For each weight: one isolated professional ecommerce packshot, using the
supplied brand artwork as reference. Preserve red woodgrain, white MEDJOOL,
gold GOLD badge, NATURE DATES serif line, square NATURES DATES logo, green WHOLE,
golden Moroccan kasbah and palm artwork and existing certification motifs.
Show a clear window containing Medjool dates. Weight labels: NET WT 150 g,
NET WT 300 g, NET WT 500 g and NET WT 600 g respectively. Entire package visible,
front/top three-quarter view, soft studio light, transparent background, no
props, floor or shadow. Do not add claims, barcodes or nutrition panels.
600 g specifically uses a clear rigid PET clamshell with a red printed label.

The final 500 g variant uses the generated 300 g pack as a visual reference:
make a distinct wider/deeper sibling with more dates, change the weight to
NET WT 500 g, and preserve the design, camera direction and transparency.

The 1 kg, 2 kg and 5 kg variants use the 500 g image as a design reference.
Prompt: generate one larger sibling with its exact NET WT 1 kg / NET WT 2 kg /
NET WT 5 kg label, matching artwork, transparent background, front/top
three-quarter perspective and a clear window of dates. Specify a deeper,
broader carton for 2 kg and a large wide bulk carton for 5 kg. No multipacks,
props, shadows, nutrition tables or barcodes. Built-in image_gen was used.

The four small formats' logistics remain unverified and displayed as available on
request. These visuals are web assets, not production print dielines or proof
of product certifications.
